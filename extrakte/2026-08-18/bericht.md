## HOUSING: FROZEN, NOT CRASHING

The consensus is that US housing does not crash while sellers sit on 2% mortgages, and only a labour-market shock changes that (https://boards.4chan.org/biz/thread/62598824). Cited against that: unemployment at 4.1%, described as healthy, versus the 20% one poster says would be needed to force boomer selling.

- Delinquencies: 2% residential against 6% commercial, claimed up 100% over two years.
- Mechanic worth knowing: sellers delist quickly to avoid "price cut" notifications on listing sites, then relist after 3 to 4 months dormant, which understates how weak demand is. The market is called "frozen" for 3 years.
- Anecdotal price gaps: $1M asked for a 1960s house with lead pipes and no AC that sold for $200K in 2004; a real Zillow listing at $125,000 offered as proof affordability exists somewhere; a French "chateau" at 350,000 € corrected in-thread to be one of 26 flats.
- Sidelined cash is the recurring position: $200k, $250k and $900k in high-yield savings, waiting.

The bank-de-risking thesis, that real estate on bank books is "shining the turd" and quietly being reduced, got the best pushback: deeply collateralized banks have every incentive to prop the market up, not abandon it. Doomer register: "you must BECOME liquidity to survive the coming collapse." Best line of the old-house sub-argument: "It's not better because it's old, it's old because it's better." Unsourced: a claim Blackstone will be forced to liquidate housing stock.

## GLOSSARY

- /smg/ - "Stock Market General," a companion recurring thread on the same board.
- Aave V4 - a decentralized lending protocol version referenced as integrating with the above testnet.
- Agorist - someone who believes in building a parallel, untaxed/unregulated economy outside state systems.
- Babylon - a protocol enabling "native" BTC staking without wrapping or bridging coins elsewhere.
- Bancor - a decentralized finance (DeFi) liquidity protocol, named as a source of lost funds by one poster.
- CARROT - a proposed Monero protocol change involving new view-key mechanics, central to the thread's relisting/privacy debate.
- Celsius - a crypto lending platform that collapsed and froze user funds, named as a source of lost funds by one poster.
- chaebol - a large, family-controlled South Korean business conglomerate (e.g. Samsung).
- Coldcard - a brand of hardware wallet for storing crypto keys offline.
- collateral/collateralized - assets (here, real estate) pledged to back a loan; central to the bank-exposure argument.
- DD - "due diligence," a poster's own research write-up on a stock, here used for the unsourced Netlist claims.
- DRAM / NAND - types of memory chips (working memory and flash storage, respectively) discussed here as a volatile trading sector.
- EOY - "end of year," used with price predictions.
- ETF - exchange-traded fund; here, a fund (FBTC) that gives BTC price exposure without self-custody.
- FCMP++ - "Full-Chain Membership Proofs," an upcoming Monero cryptographic upgrade to ring signatures/transaction privacy.
- FX - foreign exchange (currency) trading.
- halvening/halving - the periodic scheduled cut to Bitcoin's mining reward.
- ID - a per-thread poster identifier assigned by the board, not a persistent user account.
