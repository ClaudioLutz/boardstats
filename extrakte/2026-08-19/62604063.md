## Terms and slang
Smiggers/smigger - board's affectionate in-group term for /smg/ (Stock Market General) regulars.
0DTE - "zero days to expiration," an options contract expiring the same day, used for high-risk short-term bets.
Trailing stop loss - an automatic sell order that moves up with a rising price to lock in gains while limiting downside.
CTA - Commodity Trading Advisor, a type of systematic/trend-following fund whose forced buying/selling can move markets mechanically.
Liquidity sweep - a claimed pattern where price is pushed to a level (often near clustered stop-losses or options strikes) to trigger forced selling/buying, benefiting those who anticipated it.
Carry trade - a strategy of borrowing in a low-interest currency (like yen) to invest in higher-yielding assets elsewhere; unwinding it means reversing those trades, often forced by rate/currency shifts.
Factoring - a financing arrangement (mentioned re: Klarna) where a merchant gets paid upfront for a sale while the financier takes on collection risk/fee.
Bagholder/baggie - someone left holding a losing investment after a price drop; used here as "Klarna baggie."
DCA - Dollar-Cost Averaging, investing a fixed amount at regular intervals regardless of price.
Priced in - when expected future news/events are already reflected in an asset's current price, so their actual occurrence doesn't move the price further.
Rugpull - when an asset or project's backers pull support and the price collapses, often implying deception.
1pbtid-style skepticism (not explicitly used here, no exact match) - not present verbatim in this thread.
OP - "original poster," the person who started the thread.
General/smg - a recurring, long-running thread type ("Stock Market General") that continues day-to-day, distinct from one-off threads.
Anon - the default, anonymous way posters refer to each other.
