## GLOSSARY

- CCIP - Chainlink's Cross-Chain Interoperability Protocol, a product enabling communication between different blockchains.
- containment thread / general - a thread designated (formally or by convention) to confine discussion of a specific topic/coin so it doesn't spread across the board.
- cope - slang for rationalizing away disappointment or being wrong, used to describe a belief propping up morale despite bad outcomes
- Cope/copium - board slang for denial or self-soothing rationalization of a bad situation.
- crab/crabbing - a stagnant, directionless, sideways-trading market or stock.
- darknet link directory - a clearnet or onion site (like daunt or dread) that aggregates working links to darknet markets, since those links change often and get taken down.
- DTCC - the Depository Trust & Clearing Corporation, a US financial infrastructure firm; its Chainlink integration announcements are treated as major bullish catalysts for LINK.
- general - a recurring, topic-dedicated thread series (like /pmg/) that continues across days.
- General (as in "/smg/") - a recurring, long-running thread type on a given topic that gets remade repeatedly.
- get/checked - when a post's ID number has a notable repeating-digit pattern, prompting others to say "checked."
- ID - a per-thread poster identifier (not a persistent username); tracks which posts came from the same person within one thread only
- journeyman - trade/finance-adjacent jargon: a tradesperson who has completed an apprenticeship and can work independently but is not yet a master, associated with a mid-tier pay bracket.
- NAV discount - when a fund/REIT trades below the market value of its underlying assets.
- numismatics - the study/collecting of coins valued for rarity/history rather than metal content.
- OFAC - the US Treasury's Office of Foreign Assets Control, which issues and enforces sanctions.
- OP - "original poster," the person who started the thread
- smg/smiggers - the board's nickname for the Stock Market General and its regular posters.
- staking - locking up crypto to participate in network security/yield generation in exchange for rewards
