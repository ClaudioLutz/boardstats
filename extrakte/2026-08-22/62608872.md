## Terms and slang
FCMP++ - cryptographic protocol upgrade to Monero, subject of the completed audit.
SoV - "store of value," an asset held for long-term wealth preservation rather than spent; central to the thread's holding-vs-spending dispute.
OFAC - the US Treasury's Office of Foreign Assets Control, which issues and enforces sanctions.
wash trading - creating artificial trading volume (often via self-dealing) to fake demand or manipulate price, alleged here against ZEC.
agorism - an ideology/practice of conducting economic activity (legal or illicit) outside state-sanctioned channels; a "moonfag/agora-fud" hierarchy is joked about in-thread.
bagholder/baggie - someone left holding a depreciated asset after a price drop.
peg (stablecoin peg) - a mechanism that ties a token's price to a reference asset (e.g. the US dollar); the thread discusses why maintaining one reliably without a centralized issuer is very difficult.
darknet link directory - a clearnet or onion site (like daunt or dread) that aggregates working links to darknet markets, since those links change often and get taken down.
