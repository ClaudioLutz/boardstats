## Terms and slang
smg/smiggers - the board's nickname for the Stock Market General and its regular posters.
crab/crabbing - a stagnant, directionless, sideways-trading market or stock.
bagholder/bagholding - someone stuck holding a losing position they haven't sold.
FUD - "fear, uncertainty, doubt," negative claims meant to talk down an asset's price.
rugpull/rugged - when an asset or deal suddenly collapses in value or a promise is reneged on.
hopium - sarcastic term for unfounded optimism people cling to about markets.
CSP - cash-secured put, an options strategy of selling puts backed by enough cash to buy the stock if assigned.
IC (iron condor) - an options strategy combining call and put spreads to profit from low volatility.
0DTE - "zero days to expiration," options expiring the same day they're traded.
DTE - "days to expiration," shorthand for how long until an option contract expires.
ex-div date - the date after which a buyer of a stock is no longer entitled to the next declared dividend.
IPO - initial public offering, a company's first sale of shares to the public.
M2 - a measure of the money supply including cash, checking deposits, and easily convertible near-money.
short interest / shorting - here, betting against a stock's price by borrowing and selling shares (or via puts) expecting a decline.
OP - "original poster," the person who started the thread.
General (as in "/smg/") - a recurring, long-running thread type on a given topic that gets remade repeatedly.
bump - replying to a thread to keep it visible/active on the board.
1pbtid - "one post by this ID," an objection meaning a poster showed up once to push a claim and never returned.
anon - the default, anonymous way posters refer to themselves and each other.
janny - volunteer moderator ("janitor") of the board.
get/checked - when a post's ID number has a notable repeating-digit pattern, prompting others to say "checked."
bread - slang for a thread (as in "will this bread last").
