One poster proposes a tax on second/investment homes (pied-a-terre) to force boomers to sell 2020-era homes to millennials at lower prices, funded via incentives for buyers. Countered that this would crash bank and pension-fund balance sheets reliant on stable real-estate collateral. Quote: "There needs to be extra taxes on homes to offset the mortgages from 2020."
