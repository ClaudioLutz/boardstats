## Terms and slang
- LETF - leveraged ETF, a fund using derivatives/debt to multiply daily index returns.
- volatility decay - the effect where a leveraged fund's compounding losses from up/down swings erode value faster than the underlying index.
- Sharpe ratio - a measure of risk-adjusted return, comparing excess return to volatility.
- NAV discount - when a fund/REIT trades below the market value of its underlying assets.
- DCA - dollar-cost averaging, investing a fixed amount at regular intervals regardless of price.
- exit tax - a tax charged on unrealized gains when a person renounces citizenship or residency.
- managed futures - a fund strategy using long/short derivative positions across many markets, often with leverage.
- covered calls - an options strategy of selling call options against a held stock position for income.
- bagholder - someone still holding a losing position, unable or unwilling to sell.
- FUD - "fear, uncertainty and doubt," used for discouraging commentary about an asset.
- rentcuck / rentoid - derogatory board slang for someone who rents rather than owns housing.
