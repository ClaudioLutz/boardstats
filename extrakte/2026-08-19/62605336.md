## Terms and slang
BNPL - "buy now pay later," short-term installment financing (Klarna's core product).
0DTE - options expiring "zero days to expiration," same-day expiry contracts.
IC - iron condor, a multi-leg options strategy.
ITM - "in the money," an option whose strike is favorable relative to the current price.
Covered calls - selling call options against shares you own to collect premium.
Leveraged ETF (SOXL/SOXS) - exchange-traded fund using derivatives to multiply (e.g. 3x) the daily return of an underlying index/sector.
SPAC / SPAC rights/warrants - a special-purpose acquisition company (blank-check shell that merges with a private company to take it public); "rights" are a security entitling holders to shares (often at a ratio) upon merger completion.
Short interest / shorting - betting a stock will fall by selling borrowed shares.
Debt-to-GDP ratio - a country's total debt measured against its annual economic output.
divvy - slang for dividend payment.
