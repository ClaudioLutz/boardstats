## Terms and slang
- PDT - "Pattern Day Trader," a US brokerage rule restricting frequent day trading on accounts under $25,000.
- GFV - "good faith violation," a brokerage penalty for selling securities bought with unsettled funds in a cash account.
- SMA - "separately managed account," a portfolio managed individually for one client by an investment manager, as distinct from a pooled fund.
- IM - "investment manager," referenced as an entity that seeds or allocates capital to sub-managers/traders.
- AUM - "assets under management," the total capital a fund or manager controls.
- OTM/ITM - "out of the money" / "in the money," describing an option strike's position relative to the current underlying price.
- delta - an option's sensitivity to the underlying's price movement, used here as a proxy for how "in the money" a long-dated option is.
- NGMI - board slang, "not gonna make it," predicting someone's financial failure.
- picrel - "picture related," referring to an attached image as the subject of the sentence.
