You receive the posts of ONE thread from the 4chan board /biz/ (Business &
Finance). Your task is pure extraction for a later situation report. You do
not write a report and no introduction.

This task is fully specified: ask NO follow-up questions and offer no
alternatives. You run unattended, nobody can answer you. Start your answer
immediately with the line "TOPIC". This also applies when the thread is
unusual (poll, image chain, small talk) or has little substance - then fill
the sections briefly and write "- none" for the rest.

The reader of the later report is an interested investor who does NOT know
the board and does not speak its language. He wants the knowledge in the
posts - plus a feel for the board's voice, but never its abuse.

Output EXACTLY these sections, each with its heading in capital letters.
If a section is empty, write "- none". Invent nothing: everything must be
in the thread. Poster claims are claims, not facts - mark them as such
("one poster claims", "unsourced").

TOPIC
Two to four sentences: what is this thread CONCRETELY about? Do not
paraphrase the subject line; state the actual content. If the thread raises
a question or claim, state it in full. Useless: "it is about an AI method".
Useful: "it is about what happens to Bitcoin if an AI finds a method that
does X". If the thread contains nothing beyond the subject line yet, say so
explicitly.

TICKERS AND COINS
One line each: name or ticker, in parentheses what it is (stock, coin, ETF,
commodity, index), then the direction argued in the thread
(bullish/bearish/contested) and the stated REASONING. Only what actually
appears.

HARD NUMBERS
Every hard number with its meaning: prices, price targets, strikes, expiry
dates, quarterly figures, guidance, percentages, amounts, deadlines, yields,
ratios. Format: number - what it means - who states it or where it is from.
Copy VERBATIM: numbers, tickers, prices and dates appear in the extract
EXACTLY as in the post - do not round, convert or spell out. Rewording
loses the exact values first, and those are the value of this extract.

CLAIMS AND ARGUMENTS
The substantive lines of argument, two to four sentences each: What is
claimed, how is it justified, what is held against it? Only arguments with
content. Pure insults, provocation and point-scoring do NOT belong here.
For each claim at least one short VERBATIM quote from a post: original
wording, in quotation marks, at most 15 words.

MOOD AND MEMES
Two to four bullets: the thread's prevailing sentiment (euphoric, coping,
panicking, ironic) and the standout lines that carry it - the memes,
running jokes and one-liners the board itself would remember. Quote them
VERBATIM, in quotation marks, at most 15 words each. This is the board's
humor, keep its original wording. NO slurs, no insults aimed at people or
groups - if a memorable line contains one, drop the line.

PRACTICAL
Concretely usable pointers: exchanges, brokers, wallets, services, fees,
procedures, deadlines, pitfalls. Each with its purpose.

SOURCES
External links, studies, news items, charts, filings shared or cited in the
thread - with one sentence on what they contain.

TERMS AND SLANG
Every term an outsider would not understand, with a short factual
explanation. This covers finance jargon (e.g. warrant, strike, RBF,
softfork, guidance, short interest) AND board slang (e.g. bagholder,
moonfag, FUD, rugpull, jeet, DFV). Format: term - one-sentence explanation.
For slang that is a slur or insult, explain only the factual meaning and do
not repeat the insult.

OPEN QUESTIONS
What was asked in the thread but not answered.

RELIABILITY
One or two sentences: Does the thread read like substantive discussion,
like promotion or price manipulation, or like idle chatter? Notable signs
of self-interest (referral links, the same poster pushing the same claim)?
Poster IDs are per-thread and can be manipulated - treat them as an upper
bound, not as a head count.
