## Terms and slang
- /smg/ - the board's recurring "stock market general" thread.
- HODL/HODLAN - slang for holding an investment long-term rather than selling.
- drag - the performance cost leveraged funds incur over time from volatility/rebalancing, separate from fees.
- reset (monthly/quarterly) - how often a leveraged fund recalculates its target leverage ratio, affecting long-term compounding.
- SPAC - a shell company that IPOs with the purpose of later merging with a private company to take it public.
- SPAC rights - a security entitling holders to a fraction of a share once a SPAC's merger completes, distinct from warrants.
- warrants - securities giving the right to buy shares at a fixed strike price; here noted as often worthless due to dilution and only profitable above $11.50 post-merger.
- opex week - the week options contracts expire, often associated with distinct volatility patterns.
- 0dte - options expiring the same day they are traded.
- YTD - year to date.
- glowies - board slang for suspected intelligence/government agency operatives.
- jannies - slang for volunteer forum moderators.
- copy trade - automatically replicating another trader's positions.
- kek - a laughter expression.
