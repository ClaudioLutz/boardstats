## Terms and slang
- boglehead/boggle/bogglemaxxing - a passive investor who buys and holds broad index funds (e.g., VOO/VTI) rather than picking individual stocks.
- VOO and chill - shorthand for the passive strategy of buying the S&P 500 ETF (VOO) and doing nothing else.
- CAGR - compound annual growth rate, an annualized measure of investment return over multiple years.
- DCA - dollar-cost averaging, investing a fixed amount at regular intervals regardless of price.
- p/e - price-to-earnings ratio, a stock valuation metric.
- bagholder/baggot/baggie - someone still holding a losing investment position, typically mocked for refusing to sell.
- FUD - "fear, uncertainty, doubt," used to describe negative talk meant to scare people out of a position.
- exit liquidity - buyers who unknowingly provide the cash that lets earlier investors sell/profit, implying they'll be left holding losses.
- QE - quantitative easing, a central bank policy of large-scale asset purchases to inject liquidity.
- IPO - initial public offering, when a company first sells shares to the public.
- picotop - slang for the exact top of a price move, used sarcastically about market timing.
- shorts (financial) - bets that a security's price will fall, profiting from a decline.
- calls/puts - options contracts giving the right to buy (call) or sell (put) a stock at a set price.
- OP - "original poster," the person who started the thread.
- QRD - "quick rundown," a request for a short summary of a situation.
- kek - the board's generic term for laughter/amusement.
- based - board slang expressing approval or agreement with a stated opinion.
- anon - the default, anonymous identity all posters use on the board.
- tripfag - a poster who attaches a persistent identifying "trip" code to appear consistently recognizable, generally viewed with suspicion or mockery.
- 1pbtid - "one post by this ID," an objection noting a poster appeared only once in the thread and never returned, used to question their credibility.
