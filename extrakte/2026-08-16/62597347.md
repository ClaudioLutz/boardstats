## Terms and slang
- smigga/smiggers — the thread's affectionate in-group nickname for fellow posters.
- ATM (at-the-market offering) — a mechanism for a company to issue new shares directly into the market, diluting existing holders.
- ROIC — return on invested capital, a profitability/efficiency measure.
- DIX — a dark pool indicator estimating buying/selling pressure from off-exchange trades.
- circuit breaker — an automatic trading halt triggered by extreme price swings.
- carry trade — borrowing in a low-interest currency to invest in higher-yielding assets elsewhere.

- bagholder/baghold — holding a losing investment too long, often after others have sold.
- convertible bonds — corporate debt that can be converted into company equity.
