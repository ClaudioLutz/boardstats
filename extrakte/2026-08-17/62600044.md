## Terms and slang
- SMG - "Stock Market General," the recurring thread series this is part of.
- p/e - price-to-earnings ratio, a valuation metric.
- ATH - all-time high.
- EMA - exponential moving average, a technical indicator.
- MA (e.g. 20MA) - moving average, a technical indicator.
- Call option / strike - a contract giving the right to buy a stock at a fixed ("strike") price before a set expiry date.
- Divvie - slang for a dividend-paying stock or ETF.
- HOA - homeowners association fee for shared building/property maintenance.
- SPR - Strategic Petroleum Reserve, the US government's emergency oil stockpile.
- KOSPI - South Korea's main stock market index.
- TACO - board slang/meme meaning "Trump Always Chickens Out," used when a threatened action fails to materialize.
- Bagholder / baggie - someone still holding a losing investment, hoping it recovers.
- Copium - coping mechanism or self-delusion used to feel better about a bad position.
- Crab / crabbed - a market or portfolio moving sideways without net gain.
- Meltup - a rapid, euphoric market rise.
- Sneeding - internet meme term used as a nonsense reaction to a sudden stock price move.
- ETL - "English as third language," used by a non-native poster instead of ESL.
