## Terms and slang
- OPEX — options expiration day, when many derivatives contracts settle, often associated with unusual price action.
- P/E — price-to-earnings ratio, a valuation metric comparing share price to company earnings.
- guidance — a company's own forecast of its future financial performance.
- NAV — net asset value, an estimate of a company's underlying asset worth per share, often used for mining/resource companies.
- short interest / shorted — betting on a price decline by borrowing and selling an asset, referenced regarding gold.
- puts / calls — options contracts betting on a price decline (puts) or increase (calls).
- leveraged ETF (e.g., SOXL, GDXU) — a fund using debt/derivatives to amplify daily returns of an underlying sector or asset.
- crab/crabbing — board slang for a market moving sideways without clear direction.
- bagholder/"holding bags" — someone stuck holding a losing position after a price decline.
- FUD — "fear, uncertainty, doubt," spreading negative sentiment about an asset, sometimes to shake out other holders.
- slurp/slurping — board slang for buying a dip in price.
