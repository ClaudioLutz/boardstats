## GLOSSARY

- /smg/ - "Stock Market General," a companion recurring thread on the same board.
- CARROT - a proposed Monero protocol change involving new view-key mechanics, central to the thread's relisting/privacy debate.
- Clarity Act - referenced US legislative proposal aimed at establishing regulatory market-structure rules for crypto; its status ("DOA") is the thread's starting premise, but its actual provisions are not discussed.
- cope - denial or rationalization used to avoid admitting a loss.
- cope / coping - using a belief or behavior to psychologically tolerate a bad situation
- Cope/seethe - dismissive terms mocking someone's frustration or denial.
- CUSIP-6 - the first six characters of a securities identifier (CUSIP), used here to argue two securities are legally linked.
- DOA - "Dead On Arrival," meaning something (here, the Clarity Act) has effectively failed before being enacted.
- EOW - "end of week," a short-term price-target timeframe.
- EOY - "end of year," used with price predictions.
- FCMP++ - "Full-Chain Membership Proofs," an upcoming Monero cryptographic upgrade to ring signatures/transaction privacy.
- FX - foreign exchange (currency) trading.
- Gold standard - a monetary system in which a currency's value is directly linked to a fixed quantity of gold.
- GSR - Gold-Silver Ratio, how many ounces of silver equal the price of one ounce of gold.
- Junk silver - pre-1965 US coins (dimes, quarters, halves, silver dollars) that are 90% silver, traded for their melt value rather than numismatic value.
- Neoclouds - newer GPU-cloud rental companies (e.g., Nebius, CoreWeave) competing with legacy cloud providers for AI compute demand.
- SEC / CFTC - the US Securities and Exchange Commission and Commodity Futures Trading Commission, the two regulators cited as having opined on LINK's classification.
- shill - someone promoting an asset dishonestly, often for personal gain.
