## GLOSSARY

- 13F - quarterly mandatory filing that large institutional investors submit to the US SEC disclosing their equity holdings.
- Aave - decentralized platform (DeFi protocol) for lending and borrowing crypto assets.
- Babylon - protocol that enables native Bitcoin staking without transferring the coins to a centralized exchange.
- Calls/Puts - call options / put options.
- CARROT - planned new address and transaction format for Monero, named after its specification; the point of contention is the expanded view keys within it.
- Clarity Act - a bill under discussion in the US for the regulatory classification of crypto assets; referred to in the thread as dead ("DOA").
- CUSIP - unique identifying number for a security in North America.
- CUSIP / CUSIP-6 - unique North American security identifier; the first six digits identify the issuer.
- dilution unlock - the point at which previously locked shares (e.g. from employees/early investors) become sellable, which can dilute existing shareholders' stake.
- Drag - return loss on leveraged ETFs, caused among other things by daily rebalancing.
- ETF - exchange-traded fund; here a Bitcoin ETF as an indirect investment without self-custody.
- FCMP++ - "Full-Chain Membership Proofs," a new proof method in which a transaction input is obscured not just against a small group of decoys but against all past outputs on the chain.
- FSD - Full Self-Driving, Tesla's (semi-)autonomous driving assistance system.
- Hard Fork - a protocol change that forces all participants to upgrade; those who don't form a separate chain.
- LEAPS - long-dated options with more than a year to expiration.
- Optimus - Tesla's program to develop a humanoid robot.
- P/E - price-to-earnings ratio.
- Polymarket - platform for betting on real-world events; here proposed as a way to back the relisting prediction with money.
