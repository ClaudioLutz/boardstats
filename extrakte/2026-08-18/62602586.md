## Terms and slang
- PE ratio - price-to-earnings ratio, a valuation measure comparing share price to per-share profit.
- short interest - the amount/percentage of a stock's shares currently sold short, i.e. bets that the price will fall.
- puts / calls - options contracts betting a stock will fall (puts) or rise (calls).
- dead cat bounce - a brief price recovery within an ongoing downtrend, not a genuine reversal.
- ATH - "all-time high," the highest price an asset has ever reached.
- opex - options expiration, the date options contracts settle, often associated with distorted price action beforehand.
- SPR - Strategic Petroleum Reserve, the US government's emergency oil stockpile.
