## Terms and slang
- SMG - "Stock Market General," the recurring thread series this is part of.
- QRD - "quick rundown," a request to summarize a claim or DD post in short form.
- OTC / ADR - Kioxia trades "over the counter" via an American Depositary Receipt before any formal US exchange listing.
- DD - "due diligence," a poster's own research write-up on a stock, here used for the unsourced Netlist claims.
- Filtered - board slang for ignoring/blocking a poster mid-argument, used dismissively.
- p/e - price-to-earnings ratio, a valuation metric.
- ATH - all-time high.
- Call option / strike - a contract giving the right to buy a stock at a fixed ("strike") price before a set expiry date.
- HOA - homeowners association fee for shared building/property maintenance.
- SPR - Strategic Petroleum Reserve, the US government's emergency oil stockpile.
- KOSPI - South Korea's main stock market index.
- Bagholder - someone still holding a losing investment, hoping it recovers.
- Crab / crabbed - a market or portfolio moving sideways without net gain.
