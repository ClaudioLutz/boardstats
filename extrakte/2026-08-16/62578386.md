## Terms and slang
- CUSIP-6 - the first six characters of a securities identifier (CUSIP), used here to argue two securities are legally linked.
- ABL - asset-based lending facility, a type of loan secured by company assets.
- LBO - leveraged buyout, acquiring a company mostly with borrowed money.
- strike (calls) - the fixed price at which an option holder can buy the underlying stock.
- warrant - a security, often company-issued, giving the right to buy stock at a set price before expiry.
- theta - the rate at which an option or warrant loses value as it approaches expiration.
- FICA - a US payroll tax funding Social Security and Medicare.
- chapter 11 / chapter 7 - US bankruptcy types; reorganization versus full liquidation.
- NOL - net operating loss, a tax asset that can offset future taxable income.
- dark pools - private trading venues where trades occur away from public exchanges.
- gamma ramp - a feedback loop where options buying forces market makers to buy the underlying stock, pushing its price up.
- rugpull - abandoning or betraying investors/followers after building their trust.
- shill - someone promoting an asset dishonestly, often for personal gain.
- ARG - "alternate reality game," a narrative told through real-world clues, referenced here to describe RC's alleged cryptic tweets.
- cope - denial or rationalization used to avoid admitting a loss.
- wagmi - "we're all gonna make it," used ironically/hopefully.
- kek - internet slang for laughter or ironic amusement.
