## Terms and slang
CARROT - a proposed Monero protocol change involving new view-key mechanics, central to the thread's relisting/privacy debate.
FCMP++ - "Full-Chain Membership Proofs," an upcoming Monero cryptographic upgrade to ring signatures/transaction privacy.
View key - a cryptographic key that lets a third party see (but not spend) the transactions of a wallet; central to the audit/CEX-compliance debate.
Ring signature / RingCT - Monero's mechanism for obscuring which of several possible senders authorized a transaction.
Key image - a unique marker in a Monero transaction that prevents double-spending without revealing the sender.
Stealth address - a one-time address generated for each transaction so recipients aren't linkable on-chain.
RandomX (v2) - Monero's proof-of-work mining algorithm; a planned v2 change would alter it to defeat certain ASIC miners.
ASIC - specialized mining hardware built for one algorithm; loses value if that algorithm changes.
EAE attack (implied "address-reuse/exchange-linkage" attack) - a tracking technique CEX users guard against by using a fresh wallet.
KYC - "Know Your Customer," identity-verification required by regulated exchanges.
AML - "Anti-Money-Laundering" laws/regulations that shape exchange compliance requirements.
CEX / DEX - centralized exchange vs. decentralized exchange.
MCAP - market capitalization, a coin's total market value.
NGU - "number go up," slang for price-appreciation-focused thinking.
Moonfag - board slang for someone whose only interest in a coin is price speculation.
Mooncarrot - thread-specific portmanteau mocking CARROT supporters as covert price-pumpers.
Glowie - slang for a suspected government/intelligence operative or informant.
Agorist - someone who believes in building a parallel, untaxed/unregulated economy outside state systems.
FUD - "Fear, Uncertainty, and Doubt," discouraging rhetoric about an asset or project.
Cope/seethe - dismissive terms mocking someone's frustration or denial.
Polymarket - a prediction-market platform referenced as a way to bet money on a stated forecast.
EOY - "end of year," used with price predictions.
Bridge (smart-contract-based) - a mechanism to move a coin between chains via on-chain contracts rather than a centralized instant-swap service; distinguished in-thread from an "instant exchanger."
