## GLOSSARY

- /smg/ - "stock market general," the recurring thread type on /biz/ for daily market discussion.
- anon / OP / bump / thread dying - basic board mechanics: anon is any anonymous poster, OP started the thread, a bump refreshes its position, and "dead" here means discussion has stalled even if the thread technically still exists.
- Babylon - a Bitcoin staking protocol allowing native BTC staking without giving up custody.
- CNA - Certified Nursing Assistant, an entry-level healthcare role requiring a short certification course.
- CUSIP / CUSIP-6 - a unique identifier assigned to a security; the "-6" refers to the first six characters, which identify the issuer.
- DRS - Direct Registration System; registering shares directly with the transfer agent (Computershare) instead of through a broker.
- ETF - exchange-traded fund; here referring to spot Bitcoin ETFs as an alternative to self-custody.
- ETF inflows - net new money flowing into an exchange-traded fund.
- FCMP++ - a cryptographic protocol upgrade to Monero currently being audited, referenced without further technical explanation in-thread.
- fudded / FUD - "fear, uncertainty, doubt," bearish talk or rumors meant to scare holders into selling.
- general - a recurring, long-running thread dedicated to a specific topic (here, /XMR/ Monero General), rather than a one-off discussion.
- ID - a per-thread poster identifier (changes if a poster switches device/network, as one poster notes: "on mobile so new ID").
- leverage - borrowed capital used to increase the size of a trading position beyond what one's own funds would allow.
- long - a market position that profits if the price of an asset rises.
- OP - the original poster who started the thread.
- P/E - price-to-earnings ratio, a valuation metric comparing share price to company earnings.
- puts / calls - options contracts betting on a price decline (puts) or increase (calls).
- rugged/rug-pulled - a sharp, often engineered price collapse that leaves buyers holding losses.
