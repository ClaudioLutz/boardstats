## Terms and slang
- stacking - accumulating physical precious metals as a form of saving/investment.
- baker - the poster who creates/starts a new general thread using the template.
- general - a recurring, long-running thread format on a fixed topic (here /pmg/, Precious Metals General).
- OP - "original poster," the person who started the thread.
- HYSA - high-yield savings account, a bank account offering above-average interest.
- SGOV, SCHD - ticker symbols for a short-term Treasury ETF and a dividend-focused equity ETF, respectively.
- slicks - coins worn smooth from circulation, with details rubbed away.
- premium - the amount a coin or bullion piece sells for above its raw metal (spot) value.
- spot (price) - the current market price of a metal for immediate delivery/purchase.
- SLV, PSLV - ticker symbols for paper silver ETFs (SLV backed indirectly, PSLV claims physical backing), as opposed to holding physical metal.
- TOD - "transfer on death," an account designation that passes assets directly to a named beneficiary.
- 1pbtid - "one post by this ID," an objection meaning a poster showed up once to push a claim and never returned (mentioned in system instructions as an example term, not necessarily used in this thread's text; not found verbatim here, omitted from claims).
