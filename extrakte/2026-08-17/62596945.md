## Terms and slang
- GSR - Gold-Silver Ratio, how many ounces of silver equal the price of one ounce of gold.
- ASW - Actual Silver Weight, the pure silver content of a coin as opposed to its face value.
- FV - Face Value, a coin's nominal currency value as opposed to its metal value.
- Junk silver - pre-1965 US coins (dimes, quarters, halves, silver dollars) that are 90% silver, traded for their melt value rather than numismatic value.
- LMU - Latin Monetary Union, a 19th-century European currency standardization treaty referenced regarding coin sizes/purity.
- Stacktard - board slang, a mocking term for someone who accumulates ("stacks") physical gold/silver.
- 1pbtid - "1 post by this ID," a board annotation noting a poster made only a single post in the thread, often used to imply low credibility or bad-faith posting.
- Range banned - a temporary IP-range ban preventing posting from a given network/location.
- ZOG - "Zionist Occupied Government," an antisemitic conspiracy term used by several posters.
- Goyim - a Hebrew/Yiddish term for non-Jews, used pejoratively by posters in an antisemitic context in this thread.
- Whatnot - a livestream auction/shopping app mentioned as a source of silver giveaways.
- "Check 'em" - board slang/superstition referring to a post's ID number "confirming" a prediction.
- /smg/ - "Stock Market General," a companion recurring thread on the same board.
