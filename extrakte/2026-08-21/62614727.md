## Terms and slang

- ABL - Asset-Based Loan, a loan facility secured by a company's assets; referenced regarding BBBY's default with JPM.
- Change of control - a contractual trigger clause where a large enough ownership shift (here claimed at 40%+) can trigger loan default.
- Leveraged buyout - an acquisition financed largely with borrowed money.
- Ch11 / chapter 11 - a form of U.S. bankruptcy allowing reorganization rather than liquidation.
