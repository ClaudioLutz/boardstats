## Terms and slang
- CUSIP - a unique identifier code for a security; the thread's central "CUSIP-6 conundrum" theory hinges on whether two different securities share matching digits.
- ABL - asset-based lending facility, a type of loan; referenced regarding a JPM default triggered by "change of control."
- DIP - debtor-in-possession financing, loans a bankrupt company uses to keep operating during Chapter 11.
- NOL - net operating loss, a tax asset that can offset future profits; central to theories about why a shell company like DK-Butterfly-1 might be kept alive.
- Chapter 11 / Chapter 7 - US bankruptcy types; Chapter 11 is reorganization (company keeps operating), Chapter 7 is liquidation.
- Class 9 / Class 6 - creditor priority tiers in a bankruptcy plan; higher-numbered classes are typically paid after senior classes.
- Warrant - a security giving the right to buy stock at a set price before expiry, distinct from a call option.
- Strike - the price at which an option or warrant can be exercised.
- Short interest / short covering - bets that a stock will fall, and the buying pressure created when those bets are closed out.
- Section 382 (implied via "qualified indebtedness") - US tax code section limiting NOL usage after ownership changes.
