## Terms and slang
- HODL - board slang for holding through losses instead of selling.
- 0DTE - "zero days to expiration" options, contracts expiring same day.
- barbell strategy - portfolio construction combining very safe and very aggressive assets, avoiding the middle.
- drawdown - decline from a portfolio's peak to a subsequent trough.
- max pain - options-market theory that price gravitates to the strike causing option holders (as a group) the most financial loss at expiry.
- BNPL - "buy now, pay later," short-term installment lending offered by services like Klarna at point of sale.
- delinquency rate - percentage of borrowers failing to make payments on time, used as a proxy for loan-book health.
- SPR - Strategic Petroleum Reserve, a government-held emergency oil stockpile that can be drawn down to influence supply/price.
- teapot refineries - small independent Chinese oil refineries, often used to process discounted/sanctioned crude outside major state channels.
- PIKs - "payment-in-kind" securities, where interest/dividends are paid in additional securities rather than cash.
- OP - "original poster," who started the thread.
- QRD - "quick rundown," a request for a brief topic summary.
- anon - the default anonymous way posters refer to each other.
