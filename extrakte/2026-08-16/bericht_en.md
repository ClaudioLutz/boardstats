## GLOSSARY

- CARROT - planned new address and transaction format for Monero; the dispute concerns the extended view keys within it.
- Clarity Act - a US legislative proposal mentioned in the thread on market structure regulation for crypto assets.
- CUSIP - a unique identifying number for a security in North America.
- CUSIP / CUSIP-6 - North American securities identification number; the first six characters identify the issuer.
- FCMP++ - "Full-Chain Membership Proofs," a new proof method in which a transaction input is no longer obscured against just a small group of decoys, but against all previous outputs on the chain.
- Neoclouds - term for specialized cloud/data center providers that primarily rent out AI compute.
- Optimus - humanoid robot being developed by Tesla.
- SPAC/SPV - special purpose vehicle for acquiring/merging with another company.
- XMR / Monero - cryptocurrency whose transactions hide sender, recipient, and amount by default.
