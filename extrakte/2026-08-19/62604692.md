## Terms and slang
- stacking - accumulating physical precious metals (coins/bars) as an investment.
- bagholder ("baggies") - someone left holding an asset after its price has fallen, used pejoratively.
- FUD - "fear, uncertainty, doubt," used for claims meant to talk down an asset.
- DCA - "dollar-cost averaging," buying fixed amounts at intervals regardless of price.
- spot (price/deal) - the current market price of a metal, used as a benchmark for premiums.
- premium - the amount a coin/bar sells for above its melt/spot value.
- junk silver - pre-1965 US circulating coins valued for their silver content rather than collectibility.
- numismatics - the study/collecting of coins for historical or rarity value rather than metal content.
- 90% / 999 (silver purity) - shorthand for coin silver content (90% alloy vs. .999 fine).
- OP - "original post," the thread-starting post that sets the topic and links.
- baking - slang for creating/posting a new general thread, thanking the OP for doing so.
- general (as in "/pmg/") - a recurring, long-running thread type on a set topic that gets rebaked repeatedly.
- edition - the thread's changing subtitle/theme in each new bake (e.g. "Empire Edition").
- picrel - "picture related," referring to an attached image.
- kek - board laugh/expression of amusement (referenced generically as board vocabulary; not directly quoted here beyond typical usage).
