## Terms and slang
DRS (Direct Registration System) - holding shares directly in one's name via the transfer agent instead of through a broker.
Computershare - GameStop's transfer agent used for DRS.
MOASS ("Mother of All Short Squeezes") - the community's term for a hoped-for massive short-squeeze price spike.
Naked short selling - selling shares that were never borrowed or located, creating unsettled short positions.
FTD (Failure to Deliver) - when a seller fails to deliver shares by settlement date.
DTCC - the Depository Trust & Clearing Corporation, which clears and settles most US securities trades.
RRP (Reverse Repo) - a Federal Reserve facility posters track as a liquidity indicator, unrelated to GME directly.
VWAP - Volume-Weighted Average Price, a trading benchmark referenced regarding a dilution/offering window.
DSPP (Direct Stock Purchase Plan) - a company-run share-purchase plan the community warns is not equivalent to true DRS ownership.
CUSIP - the unique identifier code for a security; a warrant's CUSIP change was cited as significant.
IRC 382 / NOL - a tax code section limiting a company's use of Net Operating Losses after ownership changes.
Bagholder / baggie - someone left holding a losing position after a stock declines.
FUD - "Fear, Uncertainty, and Doubt," claimed to be spread deliberately to make holders sell.
Hedgies - short-hand for hedge funds, framed by the community as adversaries.
Towelfag/towelbaggie - slang for BBBY/BBBYQ investors (derived from "Bed Bath").
WAGMI/WGAMI - "We're All Gonna Make It," community rallying phrase.
DFV - "Deep F***ing Value," the pseudonym of the trader credited with popularizing the original GME trade.
Death cross - a technical-analysis signal where a shorter-term moving average crosses below a longer-term one, seen as bearish.
8-K / 10-Q / 10-K - SEC filings for material events, quarterly results, and annual results respectively.
Off-exchange / dark pool volume - trading volume executed away from public exchanges, tracked here as a possible sign of suppressed price discovery.
